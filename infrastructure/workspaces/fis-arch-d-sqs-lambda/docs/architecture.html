Architecture D · FIS Chaos Engineering

SQS (main + DLQ) → Lambda Consumer → DynamoDB

AWS Fault Injection Simulator — 3 chaos scenarios targeting the SQS-triggered Lambda consumer

Why concurrency-only? As of this writing, aws:fis:inject-api-internal-error and aws:fis:inject-api-throttle-error only support service: 'ec2' or service: 'kinesis' — SQS and DynamoDB are not supported service values, so FIS cannot directly inject API-level faults into this pipeline's data plane. The Lambda-extension-based invocation-error / invocation-add-delay actions were also ruled out: their exact setup (S3 layer structure, required environment variables) could not be confirmed from primary AWS documentation. All 3 scenarios below therefore use the one FIS action verified to work reliably against a Lambda target — aws:lambda:put-function-concurrent-executions — which still reproduces the operational failure modes that matter for an SQS+Lambda consumer: a stalled or degraded processor, a growing backlog, and (at sufficient duration) DLQ routing.
D-1 Consumer Outage — short

Sets the Consumer Lambda's reserved concurrency to 0 for 5 minutes. No message can be processed at all during the window.

What to verify: messages redeliver correctly within the 60s visibility timeout, no message loss, clean backlog drain once concurrency is restored.

target aws:lambda:function   ConcurrentExecutions='0' · PT5M

D-2 Consumer Outage — long (DLQ)

Sets reserved concurrency to 0 for 20 minutes — far beyond visibilityTimeout(60s) × maxReceiveCount(3) = 180s, so every in-flight message is guaranteed to exhaust its receive count.

What to verify: DLQ routing actually happens, DLQ depth is visible/alarmable, and the team has a replay procedure.

target aws:lambda:function   ConcurrentExecutions='0' · PT20M

D-3 Throughput Collapse

Limits reserved concurrency to 1 for 10 minutes. The consumer keeps running, just at a fraction of normal throughput.

What to verify: queue backlog growth rate, end-to-end processing latency, and whether alarms fire before the backlog becomes unmanageable.

target aws:lambda:function   ConcurrentExecutions='1' · PT10M
