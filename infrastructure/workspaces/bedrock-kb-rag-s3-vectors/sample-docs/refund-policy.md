# Customer refund policy (Finance)

Support agents may approve refunds up to 20,000 JPY on their own. Refunds above 20,000 JPY need approval from the support team lead.
Refunds above 200,000 JPY need approval from the finance director.

A refund is possible within 14 days of the purchase date. After 14 days only a goodwill refund is possible, up to 5,000 JPY.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days.
