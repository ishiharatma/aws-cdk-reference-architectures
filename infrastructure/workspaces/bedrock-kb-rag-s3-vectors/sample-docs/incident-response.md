# Security incident response (Security team)

Every suspected security incident must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery.
A P1 incident (confirmed data exposure or active compromise) also requires notifying the CISO immediately, at any hour.

Evidence such as logs, disk images and chat transcripts is retained for 400 days.
Affected credentials are rotated within 4 hours of confirmation. A post-incident review is held within 5 business days.
