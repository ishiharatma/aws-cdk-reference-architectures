# Expense policy (Finance)

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Reports submitted later are paid only with the approval of the finance director.

Any single expense above 50,000 JPY needs two approvers: the employee's manager and a finance reviewer.
The meal allowance on business trips is 3,000 JPY per day. Taxi rides are reimbursed only when public transport is unavailable.

Reimbursements are paid on the 25th of each month.
