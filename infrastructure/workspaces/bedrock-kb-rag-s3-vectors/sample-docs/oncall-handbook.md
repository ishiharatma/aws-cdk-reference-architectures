# On-call handbook (Infrastructure team)

The on-call rotation changes every Monday at 10:00 JST. The primary engineer carries the pager for seven days.

If the primary engineer does not acknowledge a page within 15 minutes, the page escalates to the secondary engineer.
After another 15 minutes without an acknowledgement it escalates to the infrastructure manager.

The paging tool is PagerLite. Every page needs a short note in the incident channel within 30 minutes of acknowledgement.
Handover notes are written in the shared runbook before 10:00 JST on Monday.
